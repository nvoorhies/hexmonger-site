The fight

Every blow is simulated

Your fighter has muscles, balance and a sword with weight. A backhand to the neck, a rising cut under the shield, a thrust from the feet: you draw it, and the physics decides what it does. Mail turns an edge. A gambeson soaks a club. A shield splinters plank by plank. A goblin with a broken leg goes down and stays down.

Down with your friends

Up to four, online

Invite friends from the game and take on the dungeon as a party, through Steam. Every player’s own fighter runs on their own machine, so your blows land when you throw them.

Playing alone? Three hired men-at-arms go down with you.

See it again: every fight is recorded. Press P for an instant replay at full, half or quarter speed, and move the camera anywhere to watch that backhand land again.

A new dungeon every run

The deeper you go

Every floor is generated: masonry halls with portcullises that drop behind you, winding caves, flooded sewers on several levels. Throw levers, drag crates onto pressure plates, find what opens the vault, and listen when the walls themselves start to talk. The deeper you go, the worse it gets. Die, and the run is over.

Fighter, wizard or cleric

Choose how you go down

The fighter

Sword and shield

Fences with drawn cuts and parries, and wraps the blade round a shield.

The wizard

A staff and quick fingers

Types spells as quick directional codes under pressure: a missile, a ward, a push that knocks everyone flat, a sleep that drops a room.

The cleric

Mace and prayer

Heals the party, and is no swordsman.

Enchanted weapons turn up in the chests: frost that slows, fire that sears, lightning, venom, and wounds that will not stop bleeding.

Your own fighter

Who goes down into the dark?

Build your character in a full creator: face, brows, eyes, hair, beard, skin, and clothes in the colours you choose. The fights leave their marks on them: torn cloth, blood, scars.

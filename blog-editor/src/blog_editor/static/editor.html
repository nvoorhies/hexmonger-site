      Math

      Inline: $E = mc^2$
Display:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

      LaTeX becomes MathML when the page is built, so readers need no JavaScript. A dollar sign followed by a space or digit is left alone, so “$5 and $10” is safe. Write \$ for a literal dollar.

Build a beach. Bring the tide.

Shape wet sand into castles with one finger, plant your flag, and let the sea have a go at it. The water is real, and so is the way your walls give way.

Bring the tide

The sea is not an animation

Water flows downhill, soaks into the sand, carries grains away and drops them somewhere else. Wet sand wicks, slumps and settles.

A wall that gets too wet sags. A moat that drains does its job. Your castle survives, or melts, for the same reasons a real one would.

When the tide eats your tower, it’s because the water really did undercut it.

The campaign

Twelve levels, two kinds of problem

Each level teaches a tool and gives you a new bucket mold when you finish it.

No goals unless you want them

Free play, always

Every mold you have unlocked, a whole beach and a tide button. Free play is one tap from the main menu, and the levels are there when you want them.

Made to be quiet

Sand hisses under your finger. Waves break, run up and drain away. Every sound is synthesized, with a slow, soft music bed under it all.

Cozy, not punishing

No lives and no timer in your face. The tide is the timer, and it’s slow and easy to see. If it takes your castle, build another.

Runs on what you have

Phones, tablets and the browser. Quality presets change only how it looks. The sand behaves the same on every setting.

Fair and quiet about it

What you should know before you play

  • No accounts, no sign-in. The game itself plays offline. Your beach, progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Ads between levels, never during a round. On phones and tablets, a full-screen ad can appear when you leave a finished level, at most once every twenty minutes.
  • Saying no costs nothing. If the tide takes your flag, you can choose to watch an ad to save the level, or not.
  • One purchase removes them for good. Restore Purchase brings it back on your other devices. The browser version has no ads at all.
  • Nothing about you is collected. Ads are matched to the game, not to you. Read the privacy policy.

Castles in the Sand

Build a beach. Bring the tide.

A cozy beach-building game with real water. Shape wet sand into castles with one finger, plant your flag, and let the sea have a go at it.

Play free in your browser App Store About the game

Free · no accounts · plays offline · Google Play soon

  • The sea is not an animationWater flows downhill, soaks into the sand and carries grains away. A wall that gets too wet slumps; a moat that drains does its job.
  • Twelve levels, two kinds of problemProtect your flag through a whole tide, or route the water into basins and make it stay there. Fewer grains moved means more stars.
  • Free play, alwaysEvery bucket mold you have unlocked, a full beach and a tide button. No goals unless you want them.
  • Made to be quietSand hisses under your finger, waves break and drain away, and a soft music bed sits under it all.

The studio

Small games, built with care.

Hexmonger is an independent game studio in California. We make small games with deep systems underneath: sand that behaves like sand, worlds that generate themselves, and stories that ask you to understand rather than fight.

We have spent a long time building graphics, compilers and machine-learning infrastructure elsewhere. Now we put that into games that are gentle to play and honest about what they do.

No accountsNothing to sign up for, and your progress stays on your device.
Fair adsNever in the middle of play, and every ad offer can be declined at no cost.
Plain policiesEvery game’s privacy policy is published and versioned in the open.
